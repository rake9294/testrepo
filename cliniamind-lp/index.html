FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Contact us.

Who is ClinIAMind designed for?

ClinIAMind is designed for consultants, SMEs, scientific teams, and Regulatory Affairs professionals working with literature-heavy documentation and evidence workflows.

Is ClinIAMind only for IVDR projects?

No. ClinIAMind is especially relevant for IVDR-related work, but it can also be used for broader scientific, clinical, and documentation-focused literature workflows.

Do I need technical skills to use ClinIAMind?

No. The platform is designed for non-technical users who need faster access to scientific insights and better organization of their literature projects.

What can I do with the Free plan?

The Free plan lets you discover the platform with essential features and start your first literature project at no cost — no credit card required.

Can I manage multiple projects?

Yes. Multi-project management is available in paid plans, making ClinIAMind suitable for consultants and teams handling several workstreams in parallel.

Does ClinIAMind help with regulatory documentation?

Yes. ClinIAMind is designed to support evidence-heavy workflows that contribute to structured scientific and regulatory documentation, including IVDR SVR, PER and PMS reports.
